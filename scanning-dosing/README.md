# Automated Scanning & Dosing Subsystem

Indexed, step-and-shoot ultrasonic scanning for MAGTRACK: a lead-screw stage,
a cam-driven probe and a micro-dosing pump that places couplant ahead of the probe.

- 6 mm scan grid with a 2 mm overlap for an 8 mm probe
- 4.4 N contact force from a dual-spring design
- Five safety interlocks and a six-test verification plan

[Design summary (PDF)](MAGTRACK_Scanning_Dosing_Subsystem.pdf)
